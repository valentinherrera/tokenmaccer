- Fixes a leaderboard widget that stopped responding to clicks and stopped showing new group members. Old copies of TokenMaccer (in the Trash, on the disk image, from earlier versions) were still registered with macOS and got in the way. TokenMaccer now unregisters them at every launch from the Applications folder and reloads its widgets. No files are deleted.
- The leaderboard widget names the global board "Global" (with a globe), next to your groups.
